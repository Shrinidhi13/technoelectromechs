Ratings and configuration / 02

Use nominal kVA ratings for research—not as a final selection.

Planning references on this site use nominal ratings buyers may encounter in public Mahindra Powerol product information. They do not indicate stock, final model selection, phase, duty, accessories or guaranteed availability. A rating becomes meaningful only after running load, motor starting, load sequence, power factor, site conditions and expansion are reviewed.

  • Smaller rating references: 5, 10, 15, 20, 22.5, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50 kVA.
  • Growing commercial references: 62.5, 75, 82.5, 100 and 125 kVA.
  • Industrial project references: 160, 180, 200 and 250 kVA.
  • Higher-capacity references: 320, 400, 500 and 625 kVA.
  • Separate high-capacity assessment: 1010 and 1250 kVA requirements are treated as project-specific configurations, with suitability and availability confirmed for the enquiry.
Selection safeguard

A 25 kVA DG set, 62.5 kVA diesel generator or 200 kVA Mahindra Powerol requirement cannot be selected safely from a nameplate search alone. This website does not calculate or recommend the final kVA.

Load and duty / 04

Ask the right sizing questions before choosing a generator.

Questions such as “what size generator do I need?”, “how do I calculate generator kVA?” or “what generator suits multiple motors?” require more than connected-load addition. The engineering review may need running demand, motor-starting current, start method, sequence, acceptable voltage dip, essential-load separation, operating duty and future growth.

  • kW and kVA: record the available values and power-factor information; do not treat them as interchangeable.
  • Motor loads: identify horsepower or kW, quantity, largest motor, start method and whether loads start together.
  • Prime versus standby: state the intended operating pattern instead of assuming the same rating basis applies.
  • Silent versus open configuration: describe the proposed environment, access and acoustic requirement for review.
  • Manual, AMF or ATS-related control: document the existing mains, changeover arrangement and required operating sequence.

The generator sizing guide and load-summary worksheet → organises these inputs. It is not a kVA calculator and does not replace an approved electrical design.

Installation and automation / 05

Plan the generator, site and changeover as one system.

A DG installation can involve equipment access, foundation readiness, maintenance clearance, room ventilation, exhaust routing, fuel arrangement, generator earthing, DG cabling, protection interfaces and commissioning. Exact dimensions, cable sizes, protection settings and ventilation calculations belong to the responsible project design—not a generic web checklist.

An AMF panel, or automatic mains-failure panel, monitors the selected source conditions and coordinates automatic generator starting and changeover according to the approved logic. The terms automatic changeover panel and ATS are sometimes used in related enquiries, but the actual architecture, ratings, interlocks, source sensing and transfer method must be defined.

Separate system questions

Synchronization panels, remote monitoring, load-bank testing and other specialist functions are not assumed standard inclusions. If required, identify them in the project brief so scope and technical responsibility can be confirmed.

Continue with the DG installation guide → and the AMF panel integration guide →.

AMC and service / 06

Define maintenance and breakdown scope before comparing support.

Generator AMC, DG set AMC and annual maintenance contract enquiries should begin with the make, model, rating, controller, running hours, operating environment and available service history. For a fault enquiry, share the alarm text, what happened before the issue and whether the set starts, carries load or shows abnormal temperature, smoke, voltage or sound.

  • Preventive scope: ask how inspection, battery condition, fluids, filters, coolant, operating records and scheduled visits are handled.
  • Comprehensive or non-comprehensive AMC: require inclusions, consumables, parts, attendance and exclusions to be stated in writing.
  • Repair and troubleshooting: controller, alternator, fuel-system, overheating, no-start or low-voltage symptoms may require diagnosis before responsibility is agreed.
  • Tests and spares: load testing, load-bank testing, replacements and spare parts are not automatically included unless the accepted scope says so.

This page does not claim emergency attendance, a separate authorized service centre or guaranteed spares availability. Use the generator AMC and service guide → to prepare a useful enquiry.

Application planning / 07

Match the load brief to the way the facility operates.

These are application-planning examples, not a customer list or evidence of completed installations. Each environment changes which loads are critical, how equipment starts, how long backup is expected and what access, safety, acoustic and handover constraints need attention.

MANUFACTURING

Factories and industrial units

Manufacturing plants, foundries, machine shops, CNC equipment, engineering units and sugar or food-processing facilities may involve large motors, process sequences and expansion allowances.

CRITICAL FACILITIES

Healthcare and essential loads

Hospitals, clinics and diagnostic centres should clearly separate critical circuits, changeover priorities and the responsible electrical design.

COMMERCIAL

Buildings and public-facing sites

Hotels, resorts, restaurants, banquet halls, offices, banks, retail stores, supermarkets and shopping complexes may combine lifts, HVAC, pumps, lighting and tenant loads.

EDUCATION

Schools and campuses

Schools, colleges, universities and educational campuses should identify essential buildings, laboratories, IT loads, pumps and operating schedules.

PROCESS AND LOGISTICS

Storage and distribution

Cold storage, dairy operations, warehouses, logistics hubs and petrol pumps require a clear picture of process continuity, motors and safety interfaces.

PROJECT DELIVERY

Construction and infrastructure

Contractors, construction sites, infrastructure teams, commercial-building projects, apartments, water pumps and lift-backup requirements need site, phase and responsibility boundaries.

डीजी सेटची चौकशी करण्यापूर्वी योग्य माहिती तयार ठेवा.

कोल्हापूरमध्ये डिझेल जनरेटर किंवा डीजी सेटसाठी चौकशी करताना कोणती माहिती द्यावी?

जनरेटर किंमत कोल्हापूर किंवा जनरेटर किंमत सांगली कशी मिळेल?

उपकरण, डिलिव्हरी, इंस्टॉलेशन, केबलिंग, अर्थिंग, एग्झॉस्ट, AMF, कमिशनिंग आणि कामाची मर्यादा स्पष्ट झाल्यानंतर प्रकल्पनिहाय कोटेशन तयार होते. वेबसाइटवरील एकच किंमत प्रत्येक प्रकल्पाला लागू होत नाही.

सांगलीमध्ये डिझेल जनरेटर किंवा डीजी सेटसाठी स्वतंत्र कार्यालय आहे का?

Buyer checks / 10

Common questions behind local generator searches.

Are dealer, supplier, distributor, showroom and service centre the same?

No. These terms can describe different commercial or support roles. Techno Electromechs states only the verified identity used throughout this website: an authorized Mahindra Powerol dealer based in Gokul Shirgaon, Kolhapur. Do not infer a separate distributor, showroom or authorized service-centre designation.

Does searching for a generator dealer or generator service “near me” confirm coverage?

No. Confirm the business base, exact project location, required scope and current attendance availability. Proximity wording alone does not establish a branch, stock position, assigned territory or response time.

Are all listed kVA ratings always available?

No. Ratings on this page are research references, not an inventory statement. The current model, duty, phase, configuration, accessories, commercial terms and availability must be confirmed during the enquiry.

Can this website choose the DG size for my factory, hospital or commercial building?

No. It can help organise known inputs. Final selection requires a competent review of the loads, motor starting, sequence, criticality, duty, electrical system, site conditions and applicable project requirements.

Does an AMC automatically include every repair, spare or load-bank test?

No. Preventive tasks, consumables, replacement parts, breakdown attendance, specialist repairs and testing should be stated in the accepted scope rather than assumed.

Useful project brief / 11

Send enough detail for a productive first discussion.

  • Exact location and application: city, site or industrial area, type of facility and whether it is operating, under construction or being upgraded.
  • Available load information: running loads, major motors, start methods, operating sequence, essential circuits and planned expansion.
  • Operating requirement: intended duty, expected outage pattern, control preference and any existing mains or changeover arrangement.
  • Proposed installation area: indoor or outdoor position, access, clearances, exhaust route, ventilation and distance to the electrical interface.
  • Project boundaries and timing: civil, mechanical and electrical responsibilities, required handover date and approvals still pending.
  • For service enquiries: make, model, rating, controller, running hours, service records, alarm text and current symptoms.

Share what is known; unknown items can be marked for site review. A useful enquiry is more valuable than a guessed kVA or an incomplete price comparison.
