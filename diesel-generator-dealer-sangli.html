Service-area clarity / 01

Confirm whether the project and site can be supported.

There is no Sangli branch or Sangli office represented on this page. Enquiries are handled from the Techno Electromechs base in Gokul Shirgaon, Kolhapur. Before arranging a visit or committing a schedule, the team needs the actual site location, application, project stage and required scope.

This confirmation-first approach avoids assuming that every enquiry needs the same response. A new DG supply, replacement project, AMF upgrade, installation review and service request each require different information and may involve different site coordination.

Availability note

Site visits, project delivery and service support in Sangli are subject to the equipment, location, scope, timeline and team availability being reviewed and confirmed.

Remote project brief / 02

Send enough information to make the first discussion useful.

A structured brief can establish whether a site review is the appropriate next step and which technical details remain open. Photographs or documents should only be shared through an agreed contact method and without unnecessary personal or confidential information.

BRIEF 01

Site and application

State the Sangli site location, facility type, project stage and whether the requirement is new or replacement.

BRIEF 02

Load information

List essential running loads, motors, starting methods, load sequence and any expected expansion.

BRIEF 03

Electrical controls

Describe the incoming supply, existing changeover, essential-load arrangement and whether AMF is being considered.

BRIEF 04

Project timing

Share the target date, current approvals, proposed DG location and known delivery or access constraints.

Selection pathway / 03

Explore a range, then verify the engineering inputs.

The Mahindra Powerol DG range overview → groups broad project requirements without claiming that an industry label automatically determines kVA. Final selection should consider running load, motor starting, load sequence, power factor, duty, future expansion and site conditions.

Use the generator sizing guide → to prepare those inputs. It is an educational planning aid, not a substitute for final engineering review or a promise that a particular model is available.

Commercial scope / 04

A Sangli quotation should identify logistics and site work clearly.

Generator rating is only one part of the project. A useful quotation can identify the proposed configuration and separately state controls, delivery, unloading, installation responsibilities, foundation readiness, ventilation, exhaust, earthing, cabling, commissioning and service provisions. Travel or site-coordination requirements should also be clear where they apply.

The diesel generator quotation guide → explains the information needed to request a current project-specific quotation without presenting an invented fixed price.

Site integration / 05

Resolve installation and changeover interfaces before delivery.

Site conditions affect how equipment can be delivered, positioned, ventilated, exhausted, connected, tested and maintained. The exact design remains project-specific, but an early review can identify the interfaces that need decisions from the customer, electrical contractor, civil contractor and DG team.

  • DG installation: review the principles in the DG set installation guide, then confirm their application to the Sangli site.
  • AMF integration: use the AMF panel guide to prepare utility, DG, essential-load and existing-panel information.
  • Commissioning readiness: agree access, permissions, electrical readiness, fuel, testing conditions and handover responsibilities before scheduling.

Existing equipment / 06

Service enquiries need equipment evidence as well as a location.

For AMC or breakdown enquiries, provide the DG make, model, rating, controller, serial information where available, running hours, alarm text, recent service history and a concise description of what happened. Do not repeatedly reset alarms or bypass protective interlocks without an approved technical decision.

Read the generator AMC and service guide → for the inputs used to define a preventive plan. Attendance and contract scope for a Sangli site remain subject to equipment and project confirmation.
