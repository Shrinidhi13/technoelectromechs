Range overview / 01

A project band is a starting point—not the final specification.

Techno Electromechs supports Mahindra Powerol DG requirements from compact commercial backup through major industrial projects. Grouping the range into five bands helps a buyer begin the conversation without pretending that application type alone determines the correct kVA.

The same connected load can behave differently when motors start, loads are sequenced, future expansion is planned or the operating duty changes. Final selection should therefore follow a practical load and site review.

Selection note

Every rating shown on this page is a project-navigation band. Exact model, electrical configuration, duty rating, controls, availability and installation scope are confirmed during assessment and quotation.

Selection inputs / 02

What should be reviewed before choosing a DG set?

  • Running load: the essential electrical demand that must remain supported during an outage.
  • Motor starting: starting current, starting method and whether major loads begin together or in sequence.
  • Operating duty: how the DG is expected to run and which approved rating applies to that duty.
  • Load priority: essential and non-essential circuits, changeover scope and any planned load shedding.
  • Future expansion: known additions considered without creating arbitrary oversizing.
  • Site conditions: access, foundation, airflow, exhaust, cable route, earthing and service clearance.

Use the generator sizing guide → to prepare these inputs before an engineering review.

Standard range / 03

Selected planning bands within the public 5–625 kVA range.

These bands make the public Mahindra Powerol range easier to explore. They do not replace current manufacturer information or a project-specific recommendation.

BAND 01 / COMPACT

5–30 kVA

A starting band for essential commercial loads where footprint, load priority and manual or automatic changeover need to be considered together.

BAND 02 / COMMERCIAL

35–125 kVA

A starting band for facilities with mixed lighting, HVAC, lift, equipment and essential-service loads that require a combined load review.

BAND 03 / INDUSTRIAL

160–320 kVA

A starting band for production and institutional requirements where motor starts, sequencing, duty and future expansion can materially affect selection.

BAND 04 / HIGH CAPACITY

400–625 kVA

A starting band for larger facilities where electrical coordination, delivery access, installation logistics and lifecycle planning need project-level attention.

Review the current public range on the official Mahindra Powerol product-information page ↗. Manufacturer information may change, so the quoted model and configuration must be reconfirmed for each requirement.

Project configurations / 04

1010–1250 kVA is assessed as a separate high-capacity project band.

Requirements at this scale call for detailed review of essential load, duty, electrical integration, access, unloading, ventilation, exhaust, controls, commissioning sequence and maintenance planning.

Exact model, prime or standby duty, electrical configuration, lead time and availability are confirmed during assessment and quotation. Read the 1010–1250 kVA project guide → for the decision inputs without treating the headline rating as an automatic selection.

Range questions / 07

Questions before requesting a recommendation.

Which kVA band should I start with?

Begin with connected and essential load, major motor starts, load sequence, duty and future expansion. An indicative band can organise the enquiry, but final selection follows a load and site review.

Does a larger generator automatically provide a better solution?

No. Arbitrary oversizing can create a poor technical and commercial fit. The recommendation should reflect the real load behaviour, operating duty and approved project design.

Are 1010–1250 kVA configurations selected like the standard range?

No. They are treated here as a separate high-capacity project band. Exact model, duty, electrical configuration, availability and project scope are confirmed during assessment.

Can I receive a fixed generator price from the range alone?

A project quotation depends on the selected configuration and scope, including controls, transport, site preparation, electrical work, commissioning and service requirements. The price guide explains the inputs without publishing invented figures.
