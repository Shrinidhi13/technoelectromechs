Purpose / 01

This worksheet prepares the conversation; it does not select a generator.

A generator rating cannot be responsibly chosen from one total or a general rule. The review has to consider which loads operate together, how major equipment starts, the intended duty, future expansion, site conditions and the approved electrical arrangement.

The worksheet below records what you already know and prepares a structured WhatsApp message for Techno Electromechs. It does not apply a sizing formula, calculate kVA, select a model or replace the work of the responsible electrical engineer or project consultant.

Engineering-review disclaimer

No value entered on this page is converted into a generator recommendation. Final rating, duty, controls, protection and installation requirements must be confirmed through engineering review and the approved project design.

Review inputs / 02

What information helps an engineer understand the load?

INPUT 01

Application

The facility type, supported operations and consequence of losing power provide context for the load list.

INPUT 02

Running load

Known load schedules, measured demand or equipment data help distinguish available evidence from assumptions.

INPUT 03

Starting behaviour

The largest motor or other major starting load, its start method and the operating sequence can materially affect review.

INPUT 04

Duty and duration

The intended backup or operating role and expected running pattern need to be stated rather than inferred.

INPUT 05

Expansion

Planned equipment, spare capacity expectations and changes to operating sequence belong in the project discussion.

INPUT 06

Controls and site

AMF or manual changeover intent, existing switchgear, proposed DG location and installation constraints complete the context.

After the worksheet / 04

What happens during the engineering review?

  • Clarify the load evidence: distinguish measured demand, equipment data, schedules and estimates.
  • Confirm operating sequence: identify which loads run together, which can be sequenced and which are essential.
  • Review major starts: understand motor or equipment starting methods and interactions with the supported system.
  • Define the intended duty: align the operating requirement with the applicable manufacturer and project information.
  • Check site interfaces: consider changeover, protection, cabling, earthing, ventilation, exhaust, fuel and access through the approved design process.
  • Document assumptions: record unresolved inputs before a final recommendation or quotation is issued.

Sizing questions / 05

Useful questions before choosing a DG set.

Does this worksheet calculate the required generator kVA?

No. It only organises project inputs into a message for engineering review. It does not apply a formula, recommend a rating or select a Mahindra Powerol model.

What if I do not know my running load?

State that the value is not known and share the application, available equipment information and site contact. The missing load evidence can then be identified as part of the next technical step.

Why is the largest motor important?

Major starting equipment can behave differently from its normal running condition. The equipment, known rating, start method and sequence help the engineer understand what needs closer review.

Should future expansion be included?

Yes, planned additions should be documented. Their timing, certainty and operating sequence still need engineering review before they influence a final recommendation.

Will the website keep my load information?

No. The worksheet uses no storage, upload or analytics. It prepares a WhatsApp message on your device, which you can review and choose whether to send.
