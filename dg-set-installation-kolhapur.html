Installation scope / 01

The generator is one component of the finished system.

A dependable DG installation connects equipment selection with civil readiness, ventilation, exhaust, fuel handling, cabling, earthing, controls and safe service access. Treating these as separate last-minute jobs often creates avoidable rework.

Techno Electromechs helps buyers in Kolhapur and nearby industrial areas identify the site inputs early, coordinate responsibilities and move toward a clear commissioning handover.

Before ordering

Final kVA and installation scope should follow a load review and site assessment. The quotation should clearly state what is included, what the customer prepares and which items require other approved contractors.

Site point 01

Equipment access

Check the delivery vehicle approach, unloading area, door sizes and movement path to the final position.

  • Equipment access: delivery vehicle approach, unloading area, door sizes and movement path to the final position.
  • Space and clearances: room for airflow, daily inspection, door opening and future maintenance.
  • Foundation: dimensions, level, structural readiness and coordination of anti-vibration requirements.
  • Ventilation: cool-air intake, hot-air discharge and prevention of hot-air recirculation.
  • Exhaust: routing, support, clearances and discharge location coordinated with the building.
  • Electrical route: panel positions, cable length, trench or tray route, earthing and protection interfaces.
  • Fuel arrangement: approved storage, piping, filling access and housekeeping responsibilities.

Project sequence / 03

A disciplined installation sequence reduces surprises.

STEP 01

Assess

Confirm load, application, duty, site location, control requirement and project ownership.

STEP 02

Prepare

Complete foundation, ventilation openings, exhaust route, cable route, earthing and delivery access.

STEP 03

Install

Position the DG, complete approved mechanical and electrical connections and verify clearances.

STEP 04

Commission

Perform pre-start checks, operational testing, control verification, documentation and customer handover.

Electrical integration / 04

The changeover philosophy must be clear.

The electrical scope should identify how the DG connects to the essential-load system, whether operation is manual or automatic, how the incoming supply is sensed and which protections and interlocks are required. Cable sizing, routing, termination and earthing must follow the approved design and applicable requirements.

If an automatic mains-failure sequence is required, review our AMF panel integration guide →.

Commissioning / 05

Handover should leave an operable, maintainable system.

  • Visual inspection of installation, fluids, battery, wiring and accessible connections.
  • Verification of control settings and operating sequence against the approved scope.
  • Operational trial and observation of indications, alarms and transfer behaviour where applicable.
  • Recording of equipment details, initial readings, documentation and customer responsibilities.
  • Agreement on preventive maintenance, consumables, service contacts and operating records.

Installation questions / 06

Common questions before site work.

Can you review the site before I select the DG set?

Yes. Early review helps connect kVA selection with access, ventilation, exhaust, electrical integration and the customer’s project timeline.

Who prepares the foundation and electrical cabling?

Responsibilities depend on the agreed quotation and project scope. They should be listed clearly before work starts so civil, electrical and equipment activities remain coordinated.

Can AMF integration be included?

Yes, where required. The control philosophy, panel scope, mains sensing and transfer sequence must be reviewed against the site electrical system.

Do you support installation outside Kolhapur?

South Maharashtra is the core service region. Contact Techno Electromechs with the exact location so site-visit and project support availability can be confirmed.
