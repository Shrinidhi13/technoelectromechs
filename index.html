Buyer questions / 09

Useful answers before you request a quote.

Good generator selection begins with the right questions. These are the ones buyers ask us most often.

01 What size DG set does my business need?

The right kVA depends on running load, starting current, load sequence, duty cycle and future expansion. We review these inputs before recommending a generator.

02 Do you supply and install Powerol DG sets in Kolhapur?

Yes. We support Mahindra Powerol DG projects up to 1,250 kVA and coordinate site assessment, installation, commissioning and handover in Kolhapur and nearby service areas. Exact model and configuration are confirmed during quotation.

03 Can you integrate an AMF panel with a generator?

Yes. AMF and changeover integration can be planned around your site load, mains supply and operating sequence so backup starts and transfers safely.

04 Do you provide generator AMC and service support?

Yes. Preventive-maintenance contracts, scheduled inspections, consumable replacement and breakdown coordination are available based on the generator and site requirement.

05 Which locations do you serve?

Our core service area includes Kolhapur, Sangli, Satara, Ichalkaranji and nearby industrial locations. Contact us to confirm a site visit for your exact address.
