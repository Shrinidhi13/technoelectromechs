Why preventive service / 01

A standby generator can be idle and still deteriorate.

Batteries lose capacity, terminals loosen or corrode, fluids age, filters load, hoses and belts change condition, control alarms go unnoticed and fuel-related issues can develop while operating hours remain low. The next utility failure is a poor time to discover these problems.

A generator annual maintenance contract creates an agreed inspection rhythm, defined scope, service records and a clearer path for acting on defects before they threaten availability.

Scope note

Maintenance intervals and consumables must follow the applicable manufacturer guidance, equipment age, environment, running hours and operating duty. A website cannot prescribe one interval for every DG set.

AMC scope / 02

What can a preventive visit review?

  • General condition: leakage, unusual wear, contamination, loose items, ventilation obstruction and housekeeping.
  • Engine fluids: oil and coolant level or condition, with replacement according to the approved schedule.
  • Filters and consumables: air, oil and fuel items reviewed against hours, time and site conditions.
  • Starting system: battery condition, terminals, charging arrangement and starter operation.
  • Cooling and exhaust: radiator condition, airflow path, belts, hoses, exhaust supports and visible abnormalities.
  • Controls and alarms: controller indications, stored alarms, emergency stop and operating-mode status.
  • Electrical observations: accessible connections, earthing condition, panel cleanliness and indication health.
  • Operating trial: start, run and shutdown observations using the approved test process and site permissions.

Maintenance plan / 03

Build the plan around time, hours and criticality.

Two generators of the same rating may need different attention when one operates frequently in a dusty production site and the other remains on standby in a controlled environment. A useful maintenance plan considers manufacturer recommendations, calendar age, running hours, environment, fuel condition, criticality and the customer’s permitted test windows.

INPUT 01

Equipment

Make, model, rating, serial details, controller, age and previous service history.

INPUT 02

Operation

Standby or prime duty, running hours, outage frequency, load profile and test routine.

INPUT 03

Environment

Dust, temperature, moisture, ventilation, access and site housekeeping conditions.

INPUT 04

Criticality

Consequences of failure, permitted downtime, essential loads and escalation contacts.

Service records / 04

Good records turn maintenance into evidence.

A service record should identify the equipment, date, running hours, inspection findings, work completed, consumables used, readings taken and actions still required. Repeated observations can reveal developing issues more clearly than isolated visits.

  • Keep the service report, consumable details and recommended actions together.
  • Record operating hours and significant starts, outages or abnormal events.
  • Track unresolved defects with an owner and target action date.
  • Retain controller alarm information where it supports diagnosis.
  • Update contact and escalation information when site responsibilities change.

Breakdown coordination / 05

Preventive maintenance reduces risk; it does not eliminate every failure.

When a breakdown occurs, useful first information includes the DG model and rating, controller alarm, whether the engine cranks, whether it starts but fails to accept load, recent maintenance, fuel condition and what changed immediately before the event. Accurate details help route the response more effectively.

Do not repeatedly reset alarms or bypass interlocks without an approved technical decision. Safety devices and logged faults may contain important diagnostic information.

AMC questions / 06

Questions before choosing a service plan.

Do you offer generator AMC in Kolhapur?

Yes. Techno Electromechs supports preventive-maintenance planning and generator service in Kolhapur and nearby South Maharashtra locations, subject to equipment and site review.

How often should my DG set be serviced?

The correct interval depends on manufacturer guidance, calendar time, running hours, duty and site conditions. Equipment details and service history should be reviewed before defining the plan.

Does an AMC include every spare and repair?

Coverage depends on the quoted contract. Inspection labour, consumables, scheduled replacements, breakdown attendance and major repairs should be stated clearly rather than assumed.

Can you assess a DG set without service records?

Yes, an initial condition review can establish visible status and available equipment information. Missing history should be noted because it may affect maintenance recommendations.
