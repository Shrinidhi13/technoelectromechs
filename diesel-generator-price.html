Why quotations vary / 01

A generator is part of a system, not an isolated line item.

Two enquiries for a diesel generator can refer to very different projects even when the requested kVA appears similar. One may need straightforward standby supply in a prepared room; another may include large motor starts, an AMF panel, new cabling, ventilation changes, exhaust work, difficult delivery access and a structured commissioning plan.

For that reason, this page does not publish price figures or present a universal “starting price.” Techno Electromechs prepares project quotations after the available technical and site inputs are reviewed. The quoted scope should state what is included, what is excluded and which decisions still require confirmation.

Quotation principle

Compare complete, documented scope—not only the equipment value. A lower headline can represent different duty, controls, installation responsibility, taxes, logistics or lifecycle support.

Price factors / 02

What belongs in a diesel generator quotation?

FACTOR 01

Load and duty

Known running load, major starting loads, operating sequence, standby or other intended duty and expected future expansion.

FACTOR 02

DG configuration

The approved generator model, rating basis, electrical configuration, enclosure and project-specific accessories.

FACTOR 03

Control scope

Manual changeover, AMF integration, sensing, transfer arrangement, panel interfaces, indications and documented operating logic.

FACTOR 04

Site work

Foundation readiness, ventilation, exhaust routing, fuel arrangement, cabling, earthing, protection interfaces and service access.

FACTOR 05

Delivery logistics

Distance, vehicle access, unloading conditions, movement route, lifting responsibility and coordination with the site programme.

FACTOR 06

Handover and support

Commissioning responsibility, tests, documentation, operator handover, maintenance planning and separately stated service scope.

Applicable taxes, quotation validity, payment terms, delivery assumptions, warranty terms and any exclusions should be written into the commercial offer. They should not be inferred from a website summary.

Useful inputs / 03

What should you share before asking for a price?

  • Application and location: the type of facility, city or industrial area and whether the site is operating, under construction or being upgraded.
  • Known electrical demand: available connected or running-load information and which circuits must remain supported.
  • Starting loads: the largest motors or equipment with significant starting behaviour, including the start method where known.
  • Operating intent: backup requirement, expected running pattern, critical loads and any planned expansion.
  • Control requirement: existing or proposed AMF, manual changeover, essential-load separation and customer operating priorities.
  • Proposed DG location: indoor or outdoor position, access, clearances, ventilation, exhaust route and distance to the electrical system.
  • Project boundaries: who is responsible for civil, mechanical and electrical work, along with the required timeline.

If several inputs are not yet available, begin with the load-summary worksheet →. It organises known information for an engineering discussion without calculating or recommending a generator size.

Quote comparison / 04

Compare the same scope before comparing totals.

  • Confirm the named equipment, rating basis and configuration are equivalent.
  • Separate generator supply from AMF or changeover-panel scope.
  • Identify civil, exhaust, fuel, cabling, earthing and protection responsibilities.
  • Check delivery, unloading, positioning and access assumptions.
  • Review commissioning, tests, documentation and customer handover.
  • Read taxes, payment terms, validity, delivery assumptions, exclusions and warranty wording.
  • Keep AMC, consumables, spares and breakdown support clearly defined rather than assumed.
Scope safeguard

A quotation cannot replace an approved electrical or installation design. Final cable, protection, earthing, ventilation, exhaust, fuel and control decisions remain subject to the responsible project team and applicable requirements.

Price questions / 06

Questions buyers ask before requesting a quotation.

What is the price of a diesel generator in Kolhapur?

There is no single responsible website figure for every project. The quotation depends on the reviewed load, duty, equipment configuration, controls, site work, logistics, commissioning and commercial terms.

Can I receive a quotation if I do not know the required kVA?

You can begin an enquiry without selecting a kVA. Share the application, location and available load information so the next step can be an engineering review rather than a guess.

Does the generator quotation include installation?

Only when installation is expressly included. Civil work, exhaust, fuel arrangement, cabling, earthing, panels, positioning and commissioning responsibilities should each be stated in the quotation.

Why can two quotations for a similar kVA differ?

The named model, duty basis, controls, accessories, site scope, logistics, taxes, terms and after-sales coverage may differ. Compare the written inclusions and exclusions before comparing totals.

How do I request a project-specific quotation?

Use the existing project-enquiry form to share your site, requirement and approximate range, or prepare a load summary first. The form opens a WhatsApp message for your review and does not store the details on this website.
