Your enquiry. Your choice. Clear handling.

This policy explains what information may be handled when you use this website or contact Techno Electromechs, why it is used and what choices you have.

Privacy policy / 01

Who this policy covers.

This policy applies to the Techno Electromechs website at shrinidhi13.github.io/technoelectromechs/ and to enquiries you choose to send from it. Techno Electromechs is a Mahindra Powerol authorized dealer based in Gokul Shirgaon, Kolhapur.

By using the website, you can browse the public pages without submitting your name, phone number or business requirement. Providing enquiry information is voluntary.

Information handled / 02

What information may be involved.

  • Enquiry details: your name, phone number, company, location, requirement, approximate kVA range and any additional details you enter.
  • Communication details: messages and information you later provide by WhatsApp, telephone or email, including project and service requirements.
  • Technical data: GitHub Pages and linked third-party services may process basic connection information such as an IP address, browser type, device details, referring page and access time under their own policies.
How the enquiry tools work

The website does not send the enquiry form or load-summary worksheet to a Techno Electromechs database. When you prepare a WhatsApp enquiry or load summary, the site builds a message from the details you entered and opens WhatsApp. You decide whether to send that message.

Purpose / 03

Why enquiry information is used.

Information you send may be used to respond to your request, understand the site and load requirement, prepare or discuss a quotation, arrange a visit, coordinate installation or service, provide after-sales support and maintain reasonable business records.

Techno Electromechs does not sell or rent enquiry information for third-party advertising.

External services / 04

When another service is involved.

This static website is hosted through GitHub Pages. It also uses Google Fonts and provides optional links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, telephone and email services. If you follow a link or send a message through one of those services, that provider may process information under its own privacy terms and account settings.

Only share project, personal or commercial information that is necessary for the enquiry. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through the website form.

Cookies and analytics / 05

No advertising tracker is installed here.

At the effective date of this policy, the website does not include an analytics platform, advertising pixels or a marketing cookie system. The site itself does not intentionally set advertising cookies. External resources and services may still process technical data according to their own policies.

If tracking or analytics is added later, this policy will be updated and any notice or consent control required for that setup will be considered before deployment.

Retention and security / 06

Reasonable care and limited retention.

Enquiry messages and related business records may be kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond, deliver requested work, support equipment, meet recordkeeping needs or resolve a concern. Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted or securely disposed of.

Reasonable steps are taken to protect business communications, but no internet, email or messaging method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices / 07

You remain in control of contact.

You can use the website without completing the enquiry form. You can also ask Techno Electromechs to correct or delete enquiry information it controls, or ask not to receive further follow-up, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate business, warranty, service or legal recordkeeping purposes.

This business website is intended for people making commercial or service enquiries and is not directed to children.

Updates and contact / 08

Questions about this policy?

This policy may be revised when the website, enquiry process or applicable requirements change. The effective date shown at the top will be updated when a material revision is published.
