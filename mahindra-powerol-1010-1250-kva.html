Overview / 01

High capacity needs project-level decisions.

A 1,010 or 1,250 kVA DG project is more than a generator purchase. The connected load, operating duty, motor-start behaviour, transfer arrangement, site access, ventilation, exhaust, fuel system, cabling and long-term service plan all influence the final configuration.

Techno Electromechs supports the complete decision path in Kolhapur and nearby service areas. The aim is to put the equipment recommendation inside a practical site and operating plan rather than treating kVA as the only specification.

Important sizing note

“Up to 1,250 kVA” describes supported project capability. Exact model, prime or standby duty, electrical configuration and availability are confirmed during assessment and quotation.

Project inputs / 03

What should be reviewed before selection?

  • Connected and essential load: what must remain powered and which loads may be shed.
  • Starting current: large motors, compressors, pumps, chillers and transformer energisation can shape sizing.
  • Duty requirement: standby and prime operation have different rating and usage considerations.
  • Load sequence: simultaneous starting may be avoidable when controls can stage the load.
  • Electrical integration: switchgear, AMF or synchronisation scope, protection and cable route.
  • Future expansion: known additions should be discussed without creating unnecessary oversizing.

Site readiness / 04

The installation route matters as much as the equipment room.

High-capacity equipment brings practical requirements for transport, unloading, movement into position and ongoing service access. These constraints should be reviewed before delivery, not discovered when the DG arrives.

  • Delivery access, unloading plan, turning radius and equipment movement path.
  • Foundation dimensions, load-bearing readiness and vibration considerations.
  • Cooling-air intake, hot-air discharge and separation of recirculating airflow.
  • Exhaust route, clearances and coordination with the building layout.
  • Fuel storage and piping scope according to the approved project design.
  • Power and control cabling, earthing, panel location and maintenance clearance.

Buyer questions / 06

High-capacity DG questions.

Do you support 1250 kVA DG projects in Kolhapur?

Yes. Techno Electromechs supports Mahindra Powerol DG projects up to 1,250 kVA. Final model, duty rating, configuration, lead time and site scope are confirmed during quotation.

Is 1250 kVA always the correct choice for a large plant?

No. The correct selection depends on essential load, motor starting, load sequence, duty, expansion plans and electrical coordination. A load review should precede the final recommendation.

Can AMF or other control requirements be included?

Control and transfer requirements can be reviewed as part of the project. The final architecture depends on the incoming supply, switchgear, operating sequence and critical-load strategy.

Can maintenance planning begin before commissioning?

Yes. Access, consumables, operating records and service responsibilities are best agreed during the project so the handover includes a practical lifecycle plan.
